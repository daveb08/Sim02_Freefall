Impact!
Motion is computed by numerical integration (semi-implicit Euler) so it works with air resistance too: drag = ½·Cd·ρ·A·v², opposing motion. In a vacuum (ρ = 0) drag vanishes and every object falls identically — feather and hammer land together. Playback speed slows time, not the physics.
